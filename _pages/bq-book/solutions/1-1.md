**(a)** The second derivative is $$f''(x) = 2 > 0$$ for all $$x$$, hence $$f$$ is convex.

**(b)** Setting the first derivative to zero gives

$$
f'(x) = 2x - 2 = 0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad x^\ast = 1.
$$
